## Paper claims

#### Verification of 10 schema-guided KG extraction claims (as of 2026-10-03)

Almost every number is correct. The errors are in venue and scope. Claims 8, 9 and 10 are the ones to fix before citing.

| # | Verdict | Corrected statement | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CONFIRMED | SchemaRAG, Ho, Coles, Larsson, Marshall, Bodenstab, Vozila. ACL 2026 Industry track (vol. 6, pp. 1114-1127). | Abstract: "up to an 8.8% increase in micro-F1, a 47% reduction in latency, and a 48% reduction in token costs", on healthcare and e-commerce. The 8.8% is a relative "up to" best case, and I did not check whether it is absolute or relative. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00008 |

The summarizer did not reach the camera-ready text, so a camera-ready ablation is possible. https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1674/ |
| 10 | CONTRADICTED on the number and the venue (the venue claim was right) | ACL 2026 Long (vol. 1). Disconfirming queries run:
- Searched OG-RAG for a published venue. It turned up EMNLP 2025, not the preprint-only status the arXiv text implies.
- Searched Lippolis for ESWC 2025. It turned up a workshop version, not ESWC.
- Searched for single-step vs two-step results in Ilves et al. (read the PDF). None are reported.
- Checked the AutoSchemaKG abstract and the ACL Anthology for 95%. They say 92%.

**Summary for the design doc.** Safe to cite as stated: claims 1, 2, 3 and 5 (claim 5 as a preprint). Claim 4 is safe only with "preprint, no ESWC". Claim 6 is safe only without any single-step vs two-step comparison. The caveats are:
- It is a 10-document sample.
- Triple F1 is a lower bound.
- Claim 7 needs "Medicine domain" attached to the +7.45.
- Claim 8 is a LightRAG comparison, and the ontology effect in isolation is about +3 F1 on Naive RAG.
- Claim 9 is EMNLP 2025 and its 55% and 40% are best-case relative gains with no ontology ablation. Do not cite it as evidence that the ontology causes the gain.
- Claim 10 is 92% in the abstract.
Fix the venue in claim 9 and the number in claim 10 before anything is cited.
